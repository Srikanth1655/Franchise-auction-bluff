Three mystery lots go under the hammer. Raise by ₹1M or more. Your purse is ₹100M for the whole match. Late bids add 3 seconds, up to 18 seconds total.

Star Player: +50 points. Trap: −20 points. Some cards double their outcome. A card's bid price spends purse, not points.
